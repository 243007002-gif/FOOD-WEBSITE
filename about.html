THE CHEF

The Vision of
Bibek Roy

Born in Kolkata and trained across the finest kitchens of Paris, Tokyo, and New York, Chef Bibek Roy brings together a lifetime of culinary discovery into every dish he creates.

After earning his master's degree in culinary arts from Le Cordon Bleu Paris, Bibek worked under three Michelin-starred chefs including Alain Ducasse and Joël Robuchon before opening his eponymous restaurant in 2018.

His cooking philosophy is simple: honour the ingredient, surprise the palate, and never compromise on quality — no matter what. Today, the restaurant bears his name and his promise.
